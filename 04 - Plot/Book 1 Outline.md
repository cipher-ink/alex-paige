## Act 1

- [ ] ## Chapter 1
- [ ] Introduction to Alex and the mysterious things that have been happening throughout her life.
- [ ] She attends Harmony Hills Academy which is a horrible place, except for Mr. Terrell who is oddly nice compared to the rest.
- [ ] They go on a field trip to the Museum of Natural History and Alex beings seeing things that aren’t there; exhibits changing appearance, odd poses, etc.
- [ ] After wandering away she finds herself back with the group, but suddenly her vision blurs and she sees beyond the Veil to the magical world. She freaks out as her veil breaks and she explodes, passing out.
- [ ] ## Chapter 2
- [ ] Alex wakes up and is told by Mr. Terrell that magic is real and everything weird in her life actually happened. Her parents placed a Veil to hide the magical world from her, but it broke and caused her to explode.
- [ ] Her Dad comes to pick Alex up and explains that they are traveling to their summer cabin in Maine.
- [ ] Alex falls asleep and dreams of a magical forest and a woman who looks familiar being chased. She follows as the woman casts a spell and Alex recognizes her mother. They make eye contact just as Alex wakes up from the dream.
- [ ] At the cabin, her Dad reveals that he knew about magic the entire time and everything he was doing was to protect her. Alex becomes distraught and runs away into the forest
- [ ] ## Chapter 3
- [ ] While in the forest, Alex meets Jen–a dryad–who knew her mother. Jen shows her the Glowbelles and Alex feels magic for the first time, as well as a connection to her mother.
- [ ] Hooded figures come out of the brush looking for the “Keystone” as Jen hides Alex away, telling her to rush home to her Dad, which Alex does.
- [ ] At the cabin, Alex rescues her Dad but they get into a scuffle with the assailants who are using magic. Her Dad gets injured but they manage to get away.
- [ ] ## Chapter 4
- [ ] They drive on for a bit to a random place in the woods where Alex uses an item given to her by Mr. Terrell to find the doorway to the Sanctum
- [ ] As they open the portal, they are assailed by the same people from the cabin. Alex and her Dad attempt to put up a fight, but it’s a losing battle since neither of them have magic.
- [ ] Alex flees through the doorway, but is unable to go back through after seeing that her Dad was taken hostage.
- [ ] He manages to free himself and comforts Alex before a flash of blue lightning goes off, killing him. Alex loses control of her magic and breaks the portal, causing an explosion of white.
- [ ] ## Chapter 5
- [ ] Alex finds herself in a white void and is confronted by Truth, who explains that she came here of her own volition.
- [ ] He offers her tea as Alex realizes that this is a dream. Truth asks some esoteric questions and leaves her with a message before she wakes up about Three Pillars that she must find.
- [ ] Alex wakes up in Mr. Terrell’s cabin and freaks out, yelling about her Dad as her powers become uncontrollable once more. Mr. Terrell calms her down and takes her to his gravestone as she cries.
- [ ] ## Chapter 6
- [ ] Alex grieves for weeks, but eventually gives in and begins studying the magical books that Mr. Terrell gave to her before she attends the Sanctum in the fall.
- [ ] She learns a bit about magic and attempts to channel it on her own, but when thinking about her Dad, her magic fritzes and she loses control.
- [ ] She leaves the cabin and goes to have a conversation with her Dad’s gravestone, apologizing to him and herself while promising that she was going to find her mother, if she was still out there.
- [ ] ## Chapter 7
- [ ] After coming out of the room, Mr. Terrell runs her through the basics of how magic works and uses the Truth Orbs to get her affinity: Evocation, specifically light magic.
- [ ] He teaches her more about how to use her power with a lightbulb. Alex is able to control her power for the first time, but in her excitement once again loses control, though this time she has more confidence in her abilities.


## Act 2

- [ ] ## Chapter 8
- [ ] Alex wakes up late for her first day and is rushing to get things together while Mr. Terrell calmy makes breakfast.
	
	He walks her to the top of the hill and they see that the Sanctum is right there, to which Alex chides Mr. Terrell for not telling her that much.
- [ ] They arrive at the Sanctum and go through the initial orientation ceremony, Alex noting that her name wasn’t in the Book of Families and that Mr. Terrell wasn’t fully explaining why.
- [ ] She uses the “map” Mr. Terrell gave her to find her way to the Third House, which is small in comparison to the other buildings on campus, but still the size of a mansion.
- [ ] While walking and not paying attention, she walks straight into Wyll and only he topples over. She picks him up and they introduce each other with Alex noting that he’s really skinny for his age. And meek.
	
	They realize they are in the same class and Wyll walks Alex to the room.
- [ ] When she arrives at her classroom, E22, she walks in and thinks that it looks like any old classroom. It wasn’t even any fancier than Harmony Hills. It wasn’t anything compared to the things she just saw.
	
	It calms her down a bit to know that things at least LOOK normal at first.
- [ ] Mr. Terrell arrives and class officially starts. He explains that starting at fifth year, they are assigned classes all around the campus and are free to wander about according to their schedules, however they will now have homeroom twice a day, once at the beginning and once after lunch.
- [ ] He then begins going over what they will be studying as Fifth years. For the most part, they are taking practicum classes where they will be able to cast magic under the supervision of a teacher. Their practicums are chosen based on their skillsets and affinities.
- [ ] Mr. Terrell asks if anyone can remind him of what an affinity is, calling on Alex. She freaks out for a second cause all eyes turn to her, and Trina from across the room does a motion and points to the tattoo on her arm, which Alex remembers is a crest. That gets her back on track and she answers correctly.
	
	Once they are dismissed from class, Trina comes up to Alex and introduces herself, explaining that she must be new cause Trina knows everyone in the Third Ring.
- [ ] This prompts Alex to ask and learn about the Ring system, where Trina and Wyll are explaining it as un-biased as they can, but Alex can tell there is something deeper there. Some malice towards this ring system.
- [ ] We skip through Alex’s first two classes, Algebra and Modern US History. Alex remarks that even though they are general classes, they were given a magical spin that actually made things interesting for Alex. She had no idea that The Boston Tea party was also connected to Berillium trade and taxation as an attempt to control the colonies power.
- [ ] Alex takes a bit of a break, walking around the campus before lunch as she has a bit of time. As she’s walking by, there is an explosion from one of the rooms, and Trina plus a few other third ring students she doesn’t recognize come out of the now smoking room.
	
	Trina says hello and explains that she’s doing her Enchantment Practicum class. Meaning that she’s helping to create magical tech. They were working on a basic enchantment, trying to create a Scrying Stone. Something that allows users to talk to each other from a long distance like a walkie talkie. Trina wanted to increase the range on it, and decided to alter the enchantment a little, but it backfired. She gets called back into the room by an upset looking professor, and Trina says to catch her and Wyll at lunch. Trina will show her something cool.
- [ ] Alex explores a little more before it’s time for lunch and she heads to the cafeteria, linking up with Wyll and Trina. Wyll heard all about the explosion and chides Trina for making a scene on her first day. Just because she’s good at enchanting, that doesn’t mean she should be trying to show-up the classes. Trina says that the only reason it didn’t work was because of the materials. If she were able to use the stuff at her shop at home, it wouldn’t be a problem. (Trina’s father passed away but he did enchantment for a living and left Trina with a whole workshop.)
	
	It’s a little dark for the first day of meeting, but Alex feels a kinship because of that connection.
- [ ] Alex is introduced to Professor Thorne by Mr. Terrel, who is going to help her with manifestation related things since she can learn that faster than spells until she gets a crest.
- [ ] Thorne seems nice, but a bit quirky and off, like something is bothering him. However, he’s very ernest and seems excited to help Alex learn more about her powers since it’s rare for students to start so late and not understand how their powers work. He takes some of her blood and warns her that she may notice some fluctuations in her powers for the next 24 hours.
- [ ] Alex is walking with Wyll and Trina when they tun into Tristan who mocks them as Third Ring students. Alex retorts and embarrasses him, to which one of the students looks like they are beginning to cast a spell. Tristan stops him, chiding that there are professors here and it’s not the right place. He warns Alex that they aren’t finished.
- [ ] ## Chapter 9
- [ ] It’s later in the day and Alex recieves a ransom note from Tristan. Apparently they have Wyll and are demanding that Alex meets them at night after curfew on the game fields.
- [ ] Alex considers telling Mr. Terrell but something stops her and she goes on her own and they begin dueling.
- [ ] It’s very one sided at first since Alex has no magic or training, however with some help from Trina’s magical artifact giving Alex the light she needs for her powers, she manages to get the upper hand on him and put Tristan on the ground, winning the duel.
- [ ] The guards arrive and the others manage to get away, but Alex and Tristan are given detention.
- [ ] The next day we see Alex talking more with Thorne about her manifestation. He explains what he found in her blood and identifies something unusual with the way that her magic connects to the weave. He uses that to talk to her about the loss of control she’s been having.
	
	He explains that her power isn’t broken, it’s reactive. Alex has a moment of connection where she feels understood.
- [ ] ## Chapter 10
- [ ] Alex and Tristan attend detention together in the gardens picking echoweed, supervised by the herbology professor.
- [ ] They begin chatting, the conversation turning to the Ring system since Alex doesn’t really know about it, however the echoweed reveals that Tristan thinks the whole thing is a sham and that he doesn’t feel worthy compared to his brother.
- [ ] Alex’s grief and blame about her Dad and longing to find her mother are also revealed by the echoweed, to which Tristan recognizes her mother’s name.
- [ ] While the herbology professor is distracted, they sneak off and Tristan shows Alex some old yearbooks where they find her mother won the 2006 Disk Ralley competition. Tristan’s mom was in third place that year, but she won first place in Pillar. He seems to become down when talking about his mother, to which Alex connects over.
- [ ] When they are walking back before the professor notices they are gone, they come across a body in the halls, pale and slumped on a wall. Both of them begin to panic with Alex freezing completely going back to the death of her Dad. Tristan runs off in fear, leaving Alex there alone.
- [ ] The herbology professor finds Alex there frozen and crying, unable to respond. The professor uses a plant to put her to sleep as she is beginning to lose control again.
- [ ] ## Chapter 11
- [ ] Alex wakes up in the infirmary under guard, told she can’t leave. She has no injuries, but they are keeping her under supervision
- [ ] The Dean, Headmaster, and several professors–including Mr. Terrell–arrive to interrogate her. The Dean wants to expel her immediately since she was obviously the culprit and this was a second ring student so she must have hated him.
- [ ] The Headmaster dismisses everyone else and explains that he knows Alex is innocent. He hints at larger knowledge about her and her mother, but he simply warns her to stay out of the corridors that late at night without supervision.
- [ ] Alex mentions Tristan, but the Headmaster states that he denied all involvement, making Alex feel betrayed.
- [ ] Alex spends the night in the infirmary, having a dream. It is first person and present tense. She is chained up in a cell room. Looking out the window is the same overview that she recognizes from the previous dream, but she notices an odd-looking flag that she’s never seen before.
	
	People come into her cell and begin interrogating her, asking about a keystone and whether she has awakened or not, but she doesn’t know what they are talking about.
	
	They bring a modified [[A.R.C]] into the room and hook her up to the machine, stating that they will force her to awaken if she won’t cooperate. They turn the machine on and sudden pulses surge through her body, causing Alex to jolt awake, realizing that she was glowing in her sleep.
- [ ] ## Chapter 12
- [ ] As she returns to classes, rumors have started to spread that she was culprit (most likely spread by the Dean and upper rings). It’s stated that the person had all their blood drained from their body. Her class believes that she was innocent, however their room gets trashed by the second ring kids and creates tension between her and her classmates.
- [ ] She continues to train with Mr. Terrell, and when Thorne shows up so do Wyll and Trina, wondering what she’s been up to. They sit in as Thorne talks to her more about her manifestation and brings up the loss of control again, referring to his thesis. Wyll seems to go still as he explains, and Thorne seems to notice, giving an explanation that seems to calm Wyll down, leaving Alex simply confused.
- [ ] As she attends lunch with Wyll and Trina, the announcement of the Trial rings throughout the school as assignments are revealed. They go to check, and Alex’s class will be pitted against Tristan’s class who is suspected at being the people that trashed their room.
	
	Trina notes Cayden’s name in the first ring as one of the people to watch out for. Apparently he is ruthless to lower ring students, even second.
- [ ] Mr. Terrell explains the Trials as an advancement ceremony for the ring students and is one of three ways for a student to advance: Grades and Honors, gaining recognition in a Trial, or winning an event at the Interschool Competition.
	
	The discussion quickly derails as Alex learns that the Trial is really just for the upper rings to humiliate the third ring and that no one has won in over 80 years.
- [ ] Later that night, Alex finds a mysterious note in her bag that tells her what the trial scenario will be and where to research it’s solution. The handwriting is familiar but Alex can’t place it. (It’s Thorne’s handwriting)
- [ ] ## Chapter 13
- [ ] Around a week has passed, and Alex had discussed things with Wyll and Trina, who all decided to go to the library to research the scenario and see if it’s worth believing the note.
- [ ] At some point while studying over books, Thorne happens to be walking by and asks what they are doing. He leaves to go find the librarian cause he’s looking for a book, but he gives Alex a look and a motion that tells Alex is was him that gave her that note, leaving Alex to wonder how he knows and why he told her, but she’s a bit happy someone is rooting for them.
- [ ] While searching through the library for books, Alex comes across the restricted section and notices that the lock is broken.
- [ ] She hears a crash from below and sneaks down to investigate. She sees a cloaked figure attempting to hide in the corner, but her photosensitivity lets her see the outline of the figure (Amara Sol). There aren’t any discernable details that she can make out, even being able to see them clearly. As if the shadows moved around them to hide any details. Alex instead pretends to not notice them since it seems dangerous to call attention to it.
- [ ] The figure sneaks out and Alex looks for what they might have been looking into. She makes her way to a section with shelves of artifacts on shelves, however one in particular draws her attention with a sense of familiarity. She reaches out and touches it.
	
	It suddenly turns into a book and disappears into a band of light that wraps around her wrist, leaving a black band.
- [ ] Alex sneaks back out of the restricted section and back to her friends, hiding the band under her sleeve. As she returns home Mr. Terrell and the headmaster are talking about two more victims: a teacher and another student, this time third ring.
- [ ] Alex come in and the Headmaster gives her a photo of her mother and her coven, explaining that they were a special division sent on a quest to find the Garden of Eden. As it’s described Alex recognizes it as the place from her dream. He states that her mom was captured. Throughout this conversation, Alex is distracted with her arm as she feels a burning sensation running up from the black band, but she can’t check right now.
- [ ] The Headmaster warns Alex that the same people may be after her, though he doesn’t know why. As he leaves, he hints at her knowingly preparing for the trial.
- [ ] When she gets into her room, she looks at her arm and notices a pattern crawling it’s way up her arm, which freaks her out. She decides to try and get some sleep, but it’s difficult with the burning sensation. Eventually though, she does.
- [ ] ## Chapter 14
- [ ] Alex wakes up the next morning and her Crest has fully materialized on her arm. Unable to hide it, she admits to Mr. Terrell about the restricted section incident. Mr. Terrell explains that it was most likely an anchor left behind by her mother for her to inherit. Her mom most likely hid it there until Alex was ready to inherit it. He explains how to summon her grimoire and what it is.
- [ ] She spends the time before class going through the grimoire and notices notes written down, in her mother’s handwriting as well as a note to Alex herself, which hits home that she inherited something from her mom.
- [ ] Alex spends the time between classes practicing her new spells with Wyll and Trina.
	
	Trina is fascinated with the fact that she inherited an anchor and keeps prodding Alex’s arm while Alex is still apprehensive. Wyll is silently watching.
- [ ] She manages to use Slip on her shoes to skate around the training room like ice skating, which she remembers from skating at Rockefeller center with her Dad. That thought distracts her and just before running into a wall she creates a shockwave to stop herself. She makes note to ask Mr. Terrell about it and moves on to trying the other spells.
- [ ] She casts Echo and it works correctly with a three second delay. She begins running around before Wyll notices that the image looks different. Alex turns around to see it in the image of her mom instead of her.
	
	She doesn’t know what to do but the figure turns away from her following the echo. She extends the delay to 10 seconds and does a movement so it will stay still long enough for her to see it. She turns around to see her mom and emotions come rushing forward. She tries to keep them in check like Thorne talked about, but she whispers “mom” to herself and watches the echo mimic the words perfectly in sync. This is followed by the spell suddenly exploding outwards and the room is filled with a hundred after-images of Alex’s mom looking around the room delayed from her. The images turn into her finally before disappearing and Alex falling to the ground.
- [ ] Trina and Wyll rush to help where it’s explained that was Alex’s mother. Trina is fascinated by how that works, and that maybe since it was her memory casting the spell it needed to attune to Alex, but she notices she is rambling and helps Alex up while Wyll asks how she was able to cast magic so easily.
- [ ] Its there that it’s explained Wyll refuses to use his power, and only rarely actually casts magic, which is why he fails most practicum classes.
- [ ] To erase the tension they move onto Alex’s last spell, Mark. But neither Wyll nor Trina have ever heard of it, to which Trina thinks it’s either made by her mother or a restricted spell.
- [ ] Alex goes to bed later that night and manifests her grimoire, looking through and re-reading the note her mother left for her before falling asleep with the book in her arms.
- [ ] ## Chapter 15
- [ ] Alex has a dream from her mother’s perspective.
- [ ] Alex jolts awake at the end, unable to remember specifics but understanding that her mom is in danger, wherever she is.
- [ ] ## Chapter 16
- [ ] The next day she is distracted from her classes and gets chided a bit for it. However, during training with Mr. Terrell she is suddenly more proficient with using her spells, as if she understands how to use them better at a deeper level.
	
	She asks and Mr. Terrell explains how spells passed down have a sort of muscle memory and that she will learn to use it quickly if it’s something her mother regularly used.
	
	She goes to see Thorne, but his door is locked and there’s no sign of him inside. She hasn’t actually seen him in a few days but doesn’t think much of it before going to class with Wyll and Trina.
- [ ] They decide to take a break and make their way through the maze to relax at the cafe in the center, but they are blocked by some first ring students who are calling her a blood sucker due to all the rumors going around that the people killed were drained of blood.
- [ ] Alex attempts to deny it all and pushes past them, but one of the students casts a hold spell on her.
- [ ] She suddenly starts to freak out since this was the same spell cast on her before her Dad died. The first ring student begins saying that her whole family must be murderers and horrible people, etc.
- [ ] Alex loses control of her manifestation and suddenly the gravity around her increases, breaking the spell and forcing everyone around her–including her friends–to kneel.
	
	The maze around her also begins to expand and contract before Trina helps her come to her senses.
- [ ] Alex makes fun of the first ring students for “kneeling to a commoner”. The student tries to punch her but she deflects with her training. She is about to retort again before Tristan suddenly appears.
- [ ] He states that they save it for the Trial, obviously attempting to defuse the situation. He still calls Alex names, but she recognizes the look on his face and realizes that he’s at least a little sorry.
- [ ] At the cafe, Alex reveals the dream about her mother to Wyll and Trina, who tell her that the next year they get to go on Quests. They resolve to form a Coven and search for her mother.

## Act 4

- [ ] Alex dreams from her mother’s perspective again as she uses a hairpin to pick the lock of her cuffs, letting her use magic.
	
	Alex uses her manifestation to heat the bars, bending them and letting her escape. She also uses slip on her feet and slides around eliminating the noise.
	
	As she turns a corner there is a guard and she uses Echo to send an image foreward and distract him long enough for her to come up behind cast a sleep spell on him.
	
	Alex makes her way through corridors until she finds herself in the target room. She then uses grip to climb the walls outside onto the balcony above, where she drops down. She picks the lock into the room and enters.
	
	She goes to the desk and the computer, remembering the password that they all found together.
	
	She logs in, searching through files for a word: keystone.
	
	The lights turn on and she instinctively bends the light around her, attempting to move around the desk as a figure comes in.
	
	The figure comes to the desk and notices that it’s been logged in. They look around. They snap their fingers and a loud sound echoes through the room. The figure throws a fireball at Alex, who drops her invisibility and slips in a direction, creating a pocket of pressure to push herself. She lets go of the slip spell to regain her footing.
	
	The figure is now holding a gun pointed at Alex.
	
	There is an interrogation as the reveals come out about her mother and alex being keystones. That Alex is safe in the Sanctum...for now. But they will be after her.
	
	There is a shot fired and alex wakes up.
