Alex learns about magic and loses her Dad. She starts school and learns about the new world she was thrust into and the conflict that arises from that. People start being murdered and Alex looks to solve the case. Under the murder mystery, the students have to prepare for the Trials at the end of the year and are given much less information than the nobles. They have to learn about the trial and how to solve it’s puzzle. Alex solves the murders, but it turns out to be a betrayal from someone she thought was a friend. The fallout of that causes even more hate and segregation among the school students. We end the book with news of Alex's mother and the promise that the second book will feature the Advancement tournament, which is how Alex can try to get rid of the stigma the school has for "commoner" students. 

The Trial
The Trial that they are placed in has ties with Professor Thorne. He manipulated the chosen trial because he wants information that only the Trial can reveal, being a perfect recreation of these historical moments.

The trial itself is set thousands of years ago where a village was under attack by a “vampire”. This vampire is actually one of Thorne’s ancestors who is going crazy because of their manifestation. In the original history, they were found out and Thorne’s family were ousted from nobility. This is why in the modern day Thorne is a third-string graduate.

There are two solutions to the trial. Either defeat Thorne’s ancestor or figure out how to control the curse.

Thorne wants to know how to control his manifestation before he goes overboard and starts making the same mistakes many in his family have made. He is looking to have someone figure out how to solve the trial through controlling the curse.

Thorne pulls Alex aside and gives her the details of the trial, asking her to figure out if there is a way to control the manifestation for his research since it’s a historical moment that he’s very interested in.

Alex agrees and is able to use the information to gain an advantage over the other strings who are just looking to bully the third string.

After that the murders start since Thorne is beginning to go out of control. The mystery begins.

During the trial, Alex learns the connection between Thorne and the family, but Is unable to solve the trial as the First string kills the member of Thorne’s family.
