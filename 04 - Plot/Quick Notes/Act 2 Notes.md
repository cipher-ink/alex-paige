- Alex is walking into this with fresh, hopeful determination as a contrast to the rest of the class which have already accepted defeat.
### Alex’s “Light”
During the trial, Alex will get the upper hand on Tristan, lighting a fire under the eyes of the other third rung students, who fight back and distract the other rungs long enough for the gang to find the vampire.
