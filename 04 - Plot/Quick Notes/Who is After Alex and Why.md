The people who are after Alex in the beginning chapters of the books are the [[Children of Cain]].
They are an organization that stems from Cain (of the biblical sense) and believe that their entire purpose is to rid the world of the Concept of Truth. They believe that the concept of truth is synonymous with destiny and things being foretold, meaning they are not in control of their own fates. They are looking to tip the balance between Truth and Entropy.
They only view these as Concepts instead of actual entities within the Nothing.

The Plan
They are gathering those who have been given Truth’s Blessing. They are also gathering those with light-based abilities since they also have a closer connection with Truth by default as light is the collection of all colors, symbolizing the collection of all knowledge.
They plan to gather these people and use them in a ritual to create the “Black Hole”. It’s not a literal black hole but something that in their minds will tip the scales of power.

What is the Black Hole really?
While the Children don’t completely understand what they are doing, they are looking to create a portal to the Nothing in order to give Entropy a pure connection to the material world. If they were to do this, true chaos would grab hold of the world. Whether that is good or bad is up for debate, but It would certain cause untold chaos around the material world and potentially realms beyond.

Why Alex and her Mom?
Within Alex’s lineage, someone gained Truth’s blessing. Those who gather the blessing pass down a remnant within their Blood Mark that makes it easier for those who are descended from that person to also witness Truth and gain his blessing; usually this happens in times of great emotional stress.
Because her family also has light based abilities, it’s said that her powers are the closest connection to Truth that the Children can find. Thus they want to use her lineage to open the Black Hole. The Children have come to call this combination of power the Keystone. They will refer to Alex and her Mother as Keystones.
Due to how Blood Marks work in passing down to offspring, the power of the Keystone was spread between Alex and her Mother. This is why the Children are after them both. They will need the full power of their bloodline during the ritual.
The Children have captured Alex’s mother and forced her to awaken her blessing and witness truth. Alex awakens her blessing after her Dad’s sacrifice.
