Thorne was given a book by the Children of Cain, specifically by a girl named Amara Sol. The Sol family was a prominent family and Amara graduated with honors in the Sanctum, having found and stolen the Book of Trials which dictates the trials that are within the system, how new ones are added, and how they are chosen each year by the "system". He was told about his family heritage by Amara and that he could potentially find answers in the trials. He only had to do a few things for this information. He was to help Alex and get closer to her so that he could pass information to the Children, and that under no circumstances was she allowed to come under harm by anyone until the children were ready to take her.Amara joined the Children after her family was wiped out, including her daughter. After learning about the concept of Truth and that her daughter dying was "fated" and she didn't have any free will over the situation (or so they think), she broke completely and joined. She is now one of The Chosen, a group of five individuals who are essentially "generals" of the children.

Thorne is being given a drug by the Children that helps him control his manifestation.
