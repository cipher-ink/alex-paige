## 4. Theme Through Structure

_How the theme is dramatized at each major structural beat — this is what keeps a theme from just being a line of dialogue near the end._

- **Opening image / status quo:** what does the world look like before the theme is tested?
- **Inciting incident:** how does it introduce the thematic question?
- **Midpoint:** how does the theme complicate or deepen here?
- **Low point / dark night:** how does the story test the thesis at its weakest?
- **Climax:** how is the thematic question answered (or deliberately left open) through action, not speech?
- **Ending image:** how does it mirror or invert the opening image to show what's changed?
