# Spiritual Plane
##### Alias: The Conceptual Plane
The spiritual plane is a place of pure concept. A place where thoughts, ideas, and the essence of all things exist in their truest forms. It operates outside of the laws that govern the [[Physical Plane]] and is not accessible through normal means. It is also the home of [[The Weave]]. 
### Appearance
The spiritual plane is often described in a multitude of ways as each person sees it differently. The only thing that sticks is the endless nature of it’s existence. Threads of light and color that weave together endlessly, forming a shifting and interconnected network of [[Concepts]].
