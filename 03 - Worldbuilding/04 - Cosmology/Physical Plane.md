# Physical Plane
##### Alias: The Material
The Physical Plane is the tangible and immediate realm. It is the realm of existence where mortals reside, where the mundane and magical intertwine seamlessly. This plane is the canvas for life, magic, and technology. A harmonious blend of ancient traditions and modern twists.
### Characteristics
##### [[The Veil]]
This is what divides the magical and non-magical worlds, shielding ordinary [[Laymans]] from perceiving magic.
##### Planar Connections
- [[Spiritual Plane]]
	The material is connected through to the [[Spiritual Plane]] through [[The Weave#Leylines|leylines]] which act as the bridge between the two. While one cannot traverse physically into the spiritual plane, the magic of [[The Weave|the weave]] seeps through over time, allowing [[Operators]] to channel it’s energy into magic.
- [[The Nothing]]
	While the material exists as an amalgamation of all of existence, the nothing acts as the absence of existence. It it what lies beyond everything and is unreachable from the physical plane.
- [[Sub-Planes]]
	Sub-planes are not technically considered planes by themselves, they are the liminal spaces between the planes. Sub-planes are created and destroyed as they are needed, never existing perpetually due to their unstable nature.
