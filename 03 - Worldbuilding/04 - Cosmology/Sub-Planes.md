# Sub-Planes
These are liminal spaces that exist at the intersection of the [[Physical Plane]] and the [[Spiritual Plane]]. They are considered part of [[The Nothing]] since they are not fully-fledged planes of existence. These are transitional and impermanent realities created upon a need-by-need basis.
### Features
These planes are semi-autonomous spaces, defined by their creators. Unlike full planes, they lack permanence and are often tethered to the entity responsible for their creation.
- **Duality**: these planes draw upon both the material and spiritual together, combining an abstract malleability to be crafted into whatever the user wishes.
- **Limited Scope**: They often have a fixed size or purpose, whether as a bag of holding, a battlefield for testing, or an immersive reconstruction of history.
### Creation
The creation and maintenance of these sub-planes can be very taxing depending on their purpose. Those wishing to create one must have an innate understanding of [[Classes of Magic#Conjuration|Conjuration]] and [[Classes of Magic#Enchantment|Enchantment]] magic. [[Operators]] will typically use artifacts in order to anchor these sub-planes to the physical.
##### Energy Sources
While sub-planes are sustained by magic from [[The Weave]], prolonged existence of a sub-plane can require alternative and additional infusions of magical energy.
##### Instability
Due to the chaotic nature of forming an entire realm of existence and everything that must be calculated to do this properly, it’s very easy for sub-planes to become unstable and collapse in on themselves.
