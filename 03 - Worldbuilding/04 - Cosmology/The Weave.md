# The Weave
All magic in the [[Physical Plane]] is channeled through the Weave as it’s the connecting point between the physical and [[Spiritual Plane|spiritual plane]]. It’s called that because those who can see it describe it like a nebula of stars, each one interconnected with each other and tied together through invisible strings. One plucks at the strings in order to use what mortals refer to as magic.

Many look at the interconnectedness of the Weave and think that intelligent life was modeled after the weave due to our brain’s interconnected neurons. A giant bed of information that exists throughout every living and non-living thing.
### Leylines
Leylines are the places where the Weave connects the two planes together. All magic is stronger around them, and they go through phases of opening and closing as the Earth revolves around the sun.

It’s not known why the Leylines open and close with the revolution of the sun, however theory states that the force of gravity can also affect things like the Weave, pulling it closer to the sun and this opening the Leylines the close the Earth is to it.
##### Perihelion (~January-March)
This is when the Earth is the closest to the sun during it’s revolution each year. The gravity of the sun pulls the Leylines and “stretches” them open, allowing for magic to be more readily available.
- There are holidays that were originally celebrated during this time to give thanks to the Leylines and all they provided, but over time have changed form to more “non-uniform” holidays that are more for celebrating rather than the traditional meaning.
	- [[New Year's Day]]
	- Imbolc
	- Spring Equinox
##### Aphelion (~July-September)
This is when the earth is farthest away from the sun, and when the Leylines are at their most closed. Magic is still usable, but can get noticeably weaker to those who spend time around the Leylines.
- Holidays Celebrated
	- Midsummer
	- Lammas
	- Autumn Equinox
