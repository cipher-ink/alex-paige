# Nodes
Also called Covens, these are groups of [[Operators]], primarily those employed by [[The Circle]] to go on [[Quests|quests]]. 

### Composition
Nodes are primarily composed of a team of 3-5 Operators. Very rarely will you have a node of two or less, but it has happened. Each Operator is specifically chosen to create a balanced team where each person in the Node can make up for the deficiencies and weaknesses of the others.
### Prime Nodes
Some nodes are created to be specialized in a certain field, like espionage, spying, warfare, etc. While most nodes require a balanced group, these “primes” are only created to serve a special purpose.
