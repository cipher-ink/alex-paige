# Children of Cain
Also just called the “Children”, they are an organization based around the myth of Cain from the bible. They are dedicated to the eradication of [[Truth]] and the allowance of free will.
### Founding
It’s said that the first member of the children was Cain himself, bearing the [[Mark of Cain]] after the first murder was committed. It’s said that the mark gave him the knowledge of the cosmos, allowing him to see the “truth of the universe”. Through this, Cain realized that the [[Concepts|conecpt]] of [[Truth]] was the antithesis to free will and worked for his lifetime researching how to bring about the end of it.
### Symbol

> ![[children_of_cain_logo.jpeg|148]]

The Children mark themselves with a crude sigil: the outline of an eye, an iris, struck through by a single slash. It isn’t invented branding — it’s a direct copy of the [[Mark of Cain]] itself, which is a literal object: a small bead of cracked, ancient red stone, set into the bearer’s eye socket in place of the eye it replaces. The slash across the symbol copies the scar left by that replacement, not a flaw in the stone. [[Richter Stalworth]] adopted the design because it’s simply what the thing he now carries looks like — whatever deeper meaning it may once have held is lost on him, same as the rest of the Mark’s original purpose.

Simple enough to etch into skin, wood, metal, or stone in a few strokes, which is precisely why it spread as the organization’s emblem rather than anything more elaborate.

Day to day, Richter — like every Inheritor before him — keeps the eye hidden beneath an eyepatch carrying a minor illusion-glamour, the same cheap, easily-seen-through concealment used in the Children’s cloak hoods, making it read as an ordinary eye to anyone not specifically looking past it.
### Beliefs
The children believe one thing: there is no free-will. They believe that all beings are burdened with the “illusion of choice”, only becoming puppets on strings for these deterministic forces that would push them to an unchangeable fate. Everything a person does in their life is preordained and the only people who have been given true choice are themselves, the power given to them by the [[Mark of Cain]] and it’s bearer. Through his power, the children look to bring about the end to truth and bring in [[Entropy|entropy]] to the world, which is the concept of pure chaos. They think that this will give all people of the world the free will they so covet.
##### Why?
When shown the concept of truth, it becomes obvious to them. Truth is the embodiment of all knowledge. What has happened, as well as what will, meaning that everything that will happen already has, setting it in stone so that it cannot be changed.
### Organization
##### The Inheritor
The leader of the children is the Inheritor, the person who controls the [[Mark of Cain]], who at the current time is [[Richter Stalworth]], the father of [[Emily Stalworth]] and the current president and CEO of [[RoxCorp]].
- Role:
	- They act as a ruler, prophet, and the ultimate decision maker.
	- Their words are seen as the “pure truth”, free of the influence of the concept.
- Mark:
	- Carries the [[Mark of Cain]] itself — the eye described above — and also bears the same broken-crest marker granted to the Chosen and Marked below, since the practice of branding it onto others originates with him.
##### The Chosen
These are a small group of individuals hand-picked by the Inheritor. They act as “generals” and lead the larger groups of the children in the Inheritor’s stead. They have the closest connection with the mark and are gifted powers by it’s blessing.
- Members:
	- [[Lucian Dreadmore]]
	- [[Seraphine Kael]]
	- [[Callum Vayne]]
	- [[Amara Sol]]
	- [[Bastion Krell]]
- Role:
	- Advise the Inheritor.
	- Oversee specific divisions and missions
	- Act as representatives when the Inheritor is unavailable.
	- Interpret the Inheritor’s teachings and enforce discipline among the organization.
- Mark:
	- Their crest carries a deliberate break in its pattern, the eye-and-slash symbol set into the gap — branded on personally by the Inheritor, not through ordinary Encoder work. How easily it’s spotted depends on where the break falls in their own crest’s design; sometimes buried in the linework, sometimes impossible to miss.
##### The Marked
They are the many that have been marked by the Inheritor for greatness. They are seen as “captains” that oversee the bulk of operations that go on within the organization. They are the leaders of squads of children.
- Notable Members:
	- [[Nyx Avaris]]
	- [[Draydn Kael]]
	- [[Elara Voss]]
	- [[Garrick Thorne]]
- Role:
	- Carry out key missions as the shock troops.
	- Spread the influence of the children and recruit more.
- Mark:
	- Carries the same broken-crest marking as the Chosen, set by the Inheritor’s own hand — the source of their name.
##### The Awakened
These are the masses of the children. The foot soldiers who keep the organization running. They have been drawn by the ideology of the Inheritor and have not been given any sort of blessing.
- Role:
	- Serve as the backbone for the organization, performing everyday tasks, spreading propaganda, and bolstering the group’s numbers.
	- Fight in battles under the command of either a Chosen or Marked.
	- Act as the devotees, spreading the Inheritor’s teachings and preparing for their “freedom”.
- Mark:
	- No crest-break, no brand — just a cast-silver pendant bearing the eye-and-slash symbol, worn on a cord under or over their clothing.
### Attire
Members wear whatever they like day to day — the only mandated piece is a knee-length cloak in deep violet with silver trim, worn as an accent rather than heavy ornamentation. It wraps around and clasps into a full robe, and the density of its silverwork scales with rank: plain and understated for the Awakened, increasingly elaborate up through the Chosen and Inheritor.

The hood carries a minor illusion-glamour that casts shadow over the wearer’s face — cheap, not especially powerful, and easily seen through by other magical means (other crests, implements, spells). It’s worn mostly for ceremony, but thrown over ordinary clothes whenever a bit of quiet concealment is wanted.
