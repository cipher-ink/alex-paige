# New Year’s Day
New Years is a holiday celebrated all throughout the world in the Gregorian calendar.
### Significance
This marks the opening of the [[The Weave#Leylines|leylines]] and is seen as a time for one to cleanse themselves both physically and spiritually to prepare for the new year’s magical energy.
- Those of a magical nature will say to “Pluck fresh strings”, referring to plucking at the strings of the [[The Weave|Weave]].
### History
Originally, New Years was seen as the point in time in which the [[The Weave#Leylines|leylines]] first began to open during the year, marking the start of a new “cycle”. Thus the beginning of the new year.

In modern times, most people just use it as cause to celebrate and make predictions/wishes for the new year.
### Traditions
##### String Plucking
- At midnight, individuals will pluck at symbolic strings (traditionally made of enchanted silk) as part of the ritual to align themselves with the magic of the new year. You are supposed to make a wish for the Weave to grant you luck for in the new year.
- Groups will tie these strings together to form tapestries or braids to mimic the description of the weave, symbolizing unity and shared goals for the new year.
##### Fireworks Shows
- Fireworks are set off throughout the new years as a symbol of the bursting energy of the leylines. Those that cannot hold the Cascade will often have large firework shows instead.
##### Cascade
- Cities will hold magical displays where [[Operators]] will manipulate the fresh magic of the leylines to form stunning light shows through the sky (aurora borealis), believed to rejuvenate the Weave and offer a blessing upon those who see them.
##### Forecasting
- Diviners will channel the fresh magic of the Weave in order to cast predictions on the new year. There is a lot of skepticism about whether these diviners are correct or not, but their predictions seem to spike during the time of Perihelion at the new year.
##### Cleansing
- Certain Eastern countries will have cleansing rituals where magically-charged water is used to bathe and is believed to cleanse the body and soul of the previous year’s energy.
- Communities will hold public cleansings in bathhouses.
