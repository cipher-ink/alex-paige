# Berillium
A magical substance that comes naturally in a crystal-like formation. It is a mana-conductive material, meaning that it has an innate connection to [[The Weave]] and is the main way that [[Operators]] channel their magic.
### Berillium Stones
The first form of magic was when ancient operators would simply lug around large crystals of Berillium to help them channel magic. These crystals were cumbersome and were not very efficient at channeling the magic since there was no direct connection to the operator’s blood stream. Instead, users would wear [[Blood Rings]] to cut themselves and mix their blood with the power of the stone, giving rise to [[Blood Casting]].
### Crests
Over time, the creation of [[Crests]] allowed for a user to permanently bond themselves with a Berillium mixture, letting them channel magic through their body in a much more efficient and easy-to-carry way. Since there is a direct connection to the stone and the blood, it gave rise to much easier and powerful magic.
### Technology
In the modern day, Berillium is used in most everything from magically charged batteries to cell networks able to transmit signals through [[The Weave]]. Most things of a magical nature have some kind of connection to Berillium since it became easy to convert the [[Mana 1]] stored within the stones into energy with a process known as [[Imbuing]].
### Creation
Berillium is created when lots [[Mana 1|Mana 1]] comes into direct contact with quartz crystal and becomes condensed. While very difficult to artificially create more Berillium, it is very commonplace in areas near [[The Weave#Leylines|leylines]], often being the home of large mines dedicated to it’s cultivation.
##### Magical Creatures
Magical creatures are also known to be able to create Berillium within their presence. Many [[Dragons]] and [[Wyrms]] give off so much mana, whether external or internal, that Berillium tends to spawn around their lairs.
