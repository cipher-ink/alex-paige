# A.R.C.M - Attunement Resonance Conduit Machine
Manufacturer: [[RoxCorp]]
Classification: Medical Magitech
- - -
Otherwise called the ARC, the Attunement Resonance Conduit Machine is a clinical magitech device developed by RoxCorp for use in supervised medical situations. It was designed to assist operators whose natural convergence pathway was obstructed – whether psychologically, through an abnormality, or through physical suppression – in completing the convergence process and unlocking their manifestation safely and with minimal distress.

The ARC is not a standalone device and requires and external power source typically mounted on a wheeled cart for mobility in a clinical setting. The full unit includes the conduit housing, a [[Beryllium]]-lined contact array, a mana regulation module, and a monitoring interface.
- - -
### How it Works
The ARC interfaces directly with the subject’s blood through the contact pads applied around the wrist and palms. Once attached and powered, the device draws ambient mana from the environment and channels it into the subject in small, calibrated pulses.

The pulses are designed to help in the natural rhythm of convergence – nudging the person towards the threshold rather than forcing it across. The regulation module continuously monitors the subject’s response and adjusts the intensity accordingly. The process typically takes between 1-3 hours.

Under proper clinical use, the experience is describes as uncomfortable but manageable – a persistent pressure from within, similar to the sensation of casting under duress. Side effects include fatigue, temporary sensitivity to mana, and in rare cases short term emotional dysregulation as the manifestation settles.

### Story Note – [[Book 1 Outline]]
The ARC appears in Alex’s dream sequence of her mother being interrogated. This device has been modified. The mana regulation module has been bypassed entirely, removing all safety guides. Rather than guiding the subject towards convergence, the modified unit forces mana into the subject in sustained, escalating pulses – essentially applying continuous pressure.

The [[Children of Cain]] are using it in an attempt to force the awakening of Alex’s mother, a Keystone – a process the device was never designed for and cannot safely do.
