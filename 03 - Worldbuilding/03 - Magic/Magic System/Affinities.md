# Affinities
Those who are born with magic have a natural affinity towards one of the four main [[Classes of Magic|classes of magic]]. This meaning that their connection to [[The Weave]] is stronger when channeling that kind.

Affinities do not prevent people from casting spells from other classes.

Affinities are also essential for understanding a person’s [[Manifestation|manifestation]], as the manifestation is always going to be tied to the school of magic they have an affinity with.
## Specialized Affinities
Each class of magic has it’s own sub-classes, and thus people’s affinities are more specific than just a single school. Someone can specifically have an affinity towards light, or towards summoning sentient weapons.

Finding your specialized affinity is harder than just using the [[Truth Orbs]].

## Finding your Affinity
In order to find the affinity for any [[Operators|Operator]], they need to use [[Truth Orbs]]. When someone touches an orb that they have affinity with, it will react to them.
