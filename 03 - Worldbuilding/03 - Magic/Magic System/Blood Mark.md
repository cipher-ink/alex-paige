# Blood Mark
A blood mark is much akin to a genetic marker as it’s something that one is born with and cannot change. They are integral to the creation and designs of [[Crests]] as well as being the reason magic can be inherited through multiple generations in the form of a [[Grimoire|grimoire]].
### Features
Blood marks are stored within the circulatory system, and thus have an inherent connection to [[The Weave]]. They act as the direct connecting point between [[Operators]] and the weave and what allows for people to channel [[Mana 1]] to cast spells.
### Mixing Blood Marks
Should multiple people with a blood mark procreate, there are two likely scenarios for what happens:
1. The more prominent and powerful blood mark with override the other. This typically only happens when a blood mark is much older.
2. The more likely scenario is that the blood marks mix together to create an entirely new one with the combined power of both, inheriting 50% from each, much like other genetic material.
### Crest Design
The design of a person’s crest is tied to their blood mark. When a person’s crest is initially [[Encoders|encoded]] onto them, if they already have a prominent blood mark, the design of the crest given will be overridden. Otherwise, that new design is stored into their blood mark, becoming their family crest.
### History
While it’s not well known whether blood marks have always existed within people or if they were created as a response to [[Blood Casting]], however they were discovered thousands of years ago by the [[Vernn Family]], who’s family [[Manifestation|manifestation]] was able to manipulate and control blood.

Through it’s discovery and subsequent study, the cataloging of family trees and crest designs became much easier since spells were created to translate someone’s blood into their crest design.
