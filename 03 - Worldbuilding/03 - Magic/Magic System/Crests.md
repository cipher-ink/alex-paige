# Crests
Crests are the main system through which modern [[Operators]] channel the [[The Weave|weave]] to cast spells.
### Uses
In the modern day, Crests are used as an easier alternative to spellcasting than both non-crest [[Spellcasting|spellcasting]] and simple incantation casting. Because spells can be encoded and stored onto a Crest, it makes it much easier to have access to magic. This is much akin to being able to train to use a gun rather than learning a martial art for self defense. One must simply utter the incantation and “pull the trigger”.
##### Casting
While [[Operators]] primarily only cast the stored spells on their Crest, because of the tattoo’s original function as an arcane focus for conducting magic, you are not limited to only casting the five spells encoded onto your Crest as long as you have the training to cast the spell normally.

> The sensation when one uses a Crest to channel magic from the Weave is much like drinking a warm brandy on a cold winter day. It lights a fire in the gut.
> - William Holden
### Encoding
The art of encoding a spell is done by an [[Encoders|Encoder]] and is learned through years of rigorous study. One must have an intimate understanding of [[Spell Layers|spell layers]] and the fundamentals of magic theory to properly encode a Crest onto a user.

Crests are a work of art, and thus each one is unique. While a [[Blood Mark]] can override the design of a Crest, the initial design placed onto the user is crafted by the [[Encoders|Encoder]] in the moment and cannot be done before. It is a process of allowing the [[The Weave|weave]] to flow through one’s body and have free reign over it’s own design.
##### Spell Storing
Modern-day Crests can have up to 5 spells stored on them at any given point in time. This limitation is due to the tax of mana that the Crest imparts onto the user when attempting to calculate the spell on it’s own. Normally a user would learn to calculate their spell and cast it through training, however now the crest can handle all of that work on it’s own.
- In recent years, “half-calc” Crests have been tested in order to push the 5 spell stored limit. With these Crests, the user is responsible for partial calculations of the spells and the Crest will take care of the rest.
### Appearance
Crests are made to mimic traditional spell circles. All crests start with a thick black line and then branch out into a mix of concentric circles, connecting lines and nodes, as well as arcane runes and symbols.
- Crests can be placed everywhere, but are mostly found on people’s arms. They would start with the black line at the wrist and extend upwards to around the elbow.
##### Crest Designs
Once a user is given a Crest, it’s design becomes stored within their [[Blood Mark]]. This allows for a Crest user to pass their design down through generations.
- If someone is given a Crest and they have a design stored in their [[Blood Mark]] already, the design will override the given one during their next REM cycle (the next time they sleep).
- While the power of [[Blood Mark|Blood Marks]] is inherited by the power of the mark, Crest designs will mix with genes, meaning that if two parents have a Crest, their child will gain a new design with a mixture of the two parents while.
### Structure
Crests are made of a mixture of water, iron oxide, a saline suspension, a polymer gel stabilizer, a few drops of the recipients blood, and powderized [[Berillium]].
- Water and Iron oxide create the foundational pigment and carrier for skin application.
- The saline suspension helps ensure conductivity between the Berillium and the user’s circulatory system.
- The polymer gel ensures that the mixture binds with the dermis skin layer, making it more permanent.
- A few drops of the person’s blood acts as the catalyst for the Berillium before the initial injection.
- Berillium powder connects with the user’s circulatory system and creates a channeling point for magic flowing through them.
### History
Before the creation of Crests, magic users would use Berillium stones as a focus for their magic. While magic users figured out that blood could be used to magically charge the Berillium and gave rise to [[Blood Casting]], there was still the issue of lugging around a 10lb stone everywhere you went due to the required size of the crystal.

Due to the connection of the blood making casting with Berillium easier, people learned to directly connect the stone to the user by powderizing it and creating an ink to permanently bond the Berillium to the body.

These Proto-Crests were originally only able to be used as a focus for the magic in someone’s body, still requiring years of practice to be able to use. However it made things much simpler and more portable.

An [[Encoders|Encoder]] by the name of [[Yvette Hillde]] discovered that you could alter the design of the crest to more resemble a spell circle to “store“ the spell within the crest. This would allow the user to cast that spell with much more ease than spending decades studying.

Over time the practice became more intricate, separating out the [[Spell Layers|spell layers]] to recombine and mix them together, allowing for more spells to be stored on the same crest.
