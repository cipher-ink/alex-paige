# Mana
The “lifeblood” of magic, literally. Mana is the energy that [[Operators]] gather from [[The Weave]] in order to cast spells. They channel this magic through many different ways, but the most commonplace in the modern day is using a [[Crests|crest.]]
### Flow of Mana
When mana is absorbed into the body, it gets absorbed into the circulatory system of the Operator and flows to wherever there is least resistance, much like electricity.
