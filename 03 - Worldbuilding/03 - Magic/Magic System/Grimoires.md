# Grimoires
### I. Definition
A Grimoire is a magical artifact formed at the moment a new [[Blood Mark]] is created. It serves as the physical manifestation of a bloodline’s magical legacy and evolves through the generations. Grimoires are integral to the transmission of magical theory, encoded spells, and ancestral memory.
### II. Formation and Inheritance
#### A. Creation
- A grimoire is automatically created whenever the [[Blood Mark]] is formed.
- Blood Marks may be:
	- Dominant, overriding the weaker Blood Mark
	- Merged, when two marks of equal power combine, forming a new and stronger Grimoire in return.
#### B. Lineage and Succession
- Grimoires are **passed down** through the family, typically held by the family head.
- Possession is not determined solely by bloodline but by the grimoires own recognition of the individual as a rightful successor.
### III. Access and Crest Formation
#### A. Crest Engraving
- When an individual without a [[Crests|crest]] takes ownership of the grimoire for the first time, the grimoire will engrave the family’s crest onto them overnight.
- This process can be painful, comparable to receiving a crest through normal encoding. Soreness, nausea, and vomiting are all common symptoms.
- The crest forms a permanent bond with it’s new owner.
#### B. Usage Rights
- Any individual bearing the family crest can read and study the grimoire as well as access the currently encoded spells and stored theory.
- Only the current holder of the grimoire, however, can summon it to them at their behest.
### IV. Contents of a Grimoire
#### A. Spell Theory & Notes
- Grimoires store the theoretical framework for spellcasting.
- They do not provide the ability to cast a spell outright; training and discipline are still required and such spells must be practiced for years.
- Spells must be encoded manually by a qualified [[Encoders|Encoder]] to appear within the grimoire’s pages.
- Grimoires may also contain any handwritten notes by those who have previously had ownership from the book.
#### B. Memory Inheritance
- In addition to encoded spell data and handwritten notes, Grimoires can preserve the memories of their previous owners, creating a lexicon of knowledge for the current owner to access.
- These memories are not tied to any physical pages, but are accessed through the current owner’s emotional attunement to the grimoire.
- The archive of memories grows with each generation, with no apparent limit to the information it can hold.
### V. Mechanics & Logic
#### A. Physical Form
- A grimoire is specifically a **manifestation**, not a permanent vessel.
- If physically destroyed, a new copy will reform over time to it’s previous state.
#### B. Summoning and Theft
- A grimoire can be summoned by it’s owner at any time through focus.
- While the physical grimoire can be stolen, it’s owner only needs to summon it back to them. The physical book will disappear and reappear within it’s owner’s posession.
#### C. Corruption
- The process of a grimoire becoming corrupted is vague as there have not been very many instances of it happening, though a couple things can be gleamed:
	- Only the owner or someone bearing the family crest can corrupt the grimoire.
	- Corruption may lead to warped spell data, twisted or falsified memories, and compromised crest alignment.
#### D. Sentience
- While the grimoire is not sentient in a traditional sense, it’s said that they are imbued with the will of their owners. They are able to differentiate between their owner and others, detect harm towards themselves, and act on their own according to their owner’s wishes.
